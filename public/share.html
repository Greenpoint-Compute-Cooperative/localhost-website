Put your screen on the stage

Taking you to the stage rig… This only works on the venue wifi at The School, from a laptop. Phones can share their camera instead.

OPEN THE STAGE RIG →

The rig uses its own certificate: when your browser warns you, choose Advanced → Proceed. Then press SHARE MY SCREEN and pick a tab or window; the stage operator puts you on when it's your turn. Nothing leaves the room's own network.

Nothing happened? You're probably not on the venue wifi, or the rig is off. Ask the stage desk.
